# Stat-version context for screenshot/video observations

This file records official balance changes that alter how visual stat readings
should be compared across dates. These are **version modifiers**, not
character, accessory, jewel, or monster base-stat anchors, and they do not add
coverage by themselves.

## Equipment and crystal rebalance — 2019-09-04 announcement

- **Source:** [Blustone KR — TapTap, "Crystal and equipment changes preview"](https://www.taptap.io/post/73656), posted 2019-09-04 by the account signing as `SmileV`; the post says the changes take effect after 2019-09-05.
- **Equipment:** The announcement says equipment stat values increase by 50%, fractional stat increments are removed, and upgrades after equipment-modification stage 2 (equipment level 20+) gain larger increases.
- **Crystals/jewels:** Main stats for 1–3★ crystals rise by 30%, and 4–5★ main stats rise by 50%; all substats rise by 50%. Substat upgrade amounts become fixed at the maximum rather than random within a range. Set-bonus stats are also raised, but the post gives no exact amounts.
- **Other rebalance notes:** The post says attack, defense, HP, elemental enhancement/resistance, and penetration were strengthened; crit chance, crit damage, block chance, and block-damage reduction received smaller boosts. Main-stat selection frequency was also adjusted.
- **Limits:** It does not provide per-item values, hero HP/ATK/DEF, monster stats, the exact client build at rollout, or the exact numeric set-bonus changes. Do not reverse-apply the percentages to a screenshot unless its pre/post patch state is established and the affected field is unambiguous.

### How to use this context

Any screenshot dated before 2019-09-05 is presumptively from the pre-announcement balance window, while later captures require their app/build version to be checked. This matters for dated image leads such as the Namu Hunter Gym captures (2018-04-01 and 2019-01-19) and hunter-page screenshots (2019-01-25/26); their pixels are currently inaccessible, so no stat values are inferred from them. The 2020 FAQ's exact capture date/build is not established here; do not assume which side of the change it represents.

The announcement can support later rebalance estimates and version stratification, but cannot fill any catalog denominator by itself.
