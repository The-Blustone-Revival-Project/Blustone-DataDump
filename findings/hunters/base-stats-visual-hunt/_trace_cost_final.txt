Q1.3 THE DECISIVE TEST — is supply the INVERSE of requirement?
   If the game balanced scrolls so every hero costs equal effort, then
   (traces dropped per scroll) x (collect_qty) would be constant, i.e.
   drop share would be proportional to 1/collect_qty.

   grade  n_heroes  sumTraces  mean/hero  collect_qty  mean*cost   mean/1cost
    1*      11       2852      259.27         10      2592.7    25.9273
    2*       9       1079      119.89         20      2397.8     5.9944
    3*      17        277       16.29         40       651.8     0.4074

   spread of mean*cost : 651.8 .. 2592.7   (ratio 3.98x)
   a balanced design needs ratio 1.00x.  Observed 3.98x.
   -> supply is NOT the inverse of requirement. The two are independent.

   normalised shares (of 4208) vs the two candidate designs:
     grade            1*        2*        3*
     OBSERVED      67.776%   25.642%    6.583%
     flat (equal)  33.333%   33.333%   33.333%
     inverse-cost  57.143%   28.571%   14.286%
     direct-cost   14.286%   28.571%   57.143%
   observed rank ratio 1* : 2* : 3* = 1 : 0.3783 : 0.0971
   inverse-cost would be  1 : 0.5000 : 0.2500
   -> monotonic in the WRONG direction and much steeper than either.
      The drop pool is rarity-weighted gacha supply, an independent axis.

Q2 — CAN (2^n)x10 AND 20/100/300 BOTH BE TRUE OF DIFFERENT AXES?
==============================================================================
Q2.0 the two ladders
   namu (hunter page 1.4, verified verbatim this session):
     '각 등급별로 필요한 흔적 수는 다음과 같다' — THE NUMBER OF TRACES
     REQUIRED, PER GRADE — 1* 20 / 2* 40 / 3* 80 / 4* 160, (2^n)x10
     plus the worked example '한 명당 흔적 20개가 필요한 1성 헌터를
     흔적 100개로 최대 5명을 한 번에 섭외할 수 있다'
     = floor(owned/collect_qty): 100/20 = 5. This is the NATIVE divisor.
   20/100/300 is NOT 199711. It is the vsfan wiki 'Value' column
   (List_of_Blustone_Items, capture 20190821024134, oldid 5115),
   already adjudicated NOT-COMMENSURABLE by trace-rarity-vote-2026-10-05.

Q2.1 shape test
   namu  ratios: [2.0, 2.0, 2.0] -> clean x2, and it ships a formula
   vsfan ratios: [5.0, 3.0] -> x5 then x3, no formula
   A designed threshold ladder states its rule; a price list need not.

Q2.2 the three quantities are separable, and here is the proof that they
      are three SEPARATE axes rather than one axis with a disagreement:

   AXIS 1  REQUIREMENT  (a divisor; unit = traces per hunter)
      namu 20/40/80/160. Multiplier semantics confirmed by the 100->5
      example, which matches native floor(owned/collect_qty) at Gce125c.
      Currency: none. It is a count you must hold.

   AXIS 2  SHOP PRICE  (a unit price; unit = currency per trace)
      199711 (byte-verified, 4 captures): 'Golden Hunter Traces 150 for 2'
      namu hunter page: Beckhen 3* '375개로 5개' -> 75.0 chips/trace
      -> the two AGREE EXACTLY at 75.0 fight chips per trace.
      namu item page 2.5: guild honor 700/8 (4*) and 350/8 (3*)
      -> 87.50 and 43.75 honor/trace, a 2.0x 4*:3* price step.
      NOTE the PRICE step 4*/3* = 2.0x and the REQUIREMENT step
      4*/3* = 160/80 = 2.0x are the same RATIO but different CURRENCIES
      and different physical kinds. Ratio agreement is not identity.

   AXIS 3  VALUE/ESTIMATE (the vsfan 'Value' column)
      20/100/300. Rests on a column the same page uses for
      Metron Fragment 10 / Metron Crystal 1000 = the literal FC price,
      i.e. a worth, per trace-rarity-vote section 1.2.
      Cross-check: vsfan's own FC prices are 75 per 3* trace and
      37.5 per 2* trace (ratios 1:2), while its Value column says
      100 and 300 (ratio 1:3). A price column and a worth column
      cannot both be right about the same shop.

Q2.3 VERDICT
   On the SAME axis namu and vsfan are INCOMPATIBLE: ratio 1.00 / 2.50 /
   3.75 is not a constant scale factor, so at most one can be the
   requirement. But they are on DIFFERENT axes, so there is no actual
   contradiction to resolve — the project simply has not been tracking
   which axis each number lives on. That is the real defect.

       (67.8 / 25.6 / 6.6 %% by grade), whereas the exchange is a

   collect_qty is 4, and Lapis's 358 does NOT mean 358.
