**Use:** A separate puzzle-game title by VisualShower has public Monster Info
panels that expose numeric HP and ATK for some creatures whose names or art may
overlap Blustone's setting. These are **cross-title clues only**. MON BLAST! is
a distinct match-puzzle PvP app (`com.vshower.prd_pf`); Blustone is
`com.vshower.prd_sr`. Its card level, rules, scaling, and balance have not been
shown to match Blustone's server-authoritative enemy data. **No value below
counts toward the 70% Blustone monster target.**

## Distinct-game verification

- [QooApp's MON BLAST! listing](https://m-apps.qoo-app.com/ko/app/13132) labels
  the app as a puzzle title by VisualShower and lists package ID
  `com.vshower.prd_pf`.
- The Monster Info frames are from official-channel `Mon Blast!` clips, and the
  cards display puzzle-specific effects (board disruption, team-ATK strikes).
  That gameplay context differs from Blustone's stage enemy data.
- The Blustone catalogue still has 22 enemies `M001`–`M021` and `M100`, each
  with unknown display identity and server-authoritative HP/ATK/DEF. No direct
  name/ID crosswalk has been found.

**Follow-up rule:** Only reconsider these as Blustone evidence if a source maps a
MON BLAST! card to a specific Blustone monster and independently establishes
that the stat values and level scale are shared. Until then, keep this file
separate from coverage counts.
