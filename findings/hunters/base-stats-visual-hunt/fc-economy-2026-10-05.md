### 2.2 The 255 anomaly (551–720 band)

- Raw reading: rank band **551–720** pays fight chips 1000 + **녹색 만다 255**.
- The surrounding manda run descends ...275 (321–420) → 250 (421–550) →
  **255** (551–720) → 200 (721–950) → 175... The 255 breaks the monotonic
  descent (250→200 expected at this step).
- Source note (A §5) preserves it literally as "sic — breaks the descending
  250→200 run; preserved literally, likely wiki typo, do not smooth".
- Treatment: keep **255** in every transcription; never smooth to 225/250/200
  or interpolate. Flag for any future rebalance use as a probable wiki typo
  with unknown intended value. No second source confirms or corrects it
  (B has no independent transcription; C is empty).
