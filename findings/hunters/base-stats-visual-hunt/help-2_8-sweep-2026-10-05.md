## 0.3 Record shape in the 2.8.0.1 block

- Key order is `type`, `id`, `res`\|`res_local`, `title`, `desc1` [`desc2`].
- **No `CONTENTS12_*`..`CONTENTS15_*` record has a `desc2`.** All 22 carry
  `title` + `desc1` only. (`desc2` exists elsewhere in the corpus — `CONTENTS11_8`,
  `CONTENTS1_2`, `CONTENTS1_4`..`1_6`, `CONTENTS7_1` are the seven that have it.)
- Locale keys were renamed relative to 1.4.4.1: `ko/en/jp/zh/zt` → **`KO/EN/JA/SC/TC`**
  (`zh`→`SC`, `zt`→`TC`, `jp`→`JA`), order preserved.
- **Resource-key migration is partial and mid-corpus.** Of the 73 shared ids, **40**
  change shape: 1.4.4.1's single `res: "G_LI<n>_<m>"` becomes a per-locale
  `res_local` map in 2.8.0.1, **always in the same direction** — no record went the
  other way. Corpus-wide: 1.4.4.1 is 65 `res` + 8 `res_local`; 2.8.0.1 is 36 `res` +
  56 `res_local` + **3 `res: ""`**. The `res_local` set is not contiguous — it
  starts at `CONTENTS2_3`/`2_5`, skips `4_2`/`4_3`/`4_5`, resumes across `7`–`9`,
  skips `11_1`/`11_4`–`11_8`, and alternates freely in `10`. Both shapes occur
  inside the `CONTENTS12_*`..`CONTENTS15_*` block:
  `CONTENTS12_5/12_6`, `CONTENTS13_2/13_3/13_4`, `CONTENTS15_4/15_5/15_7` use
  `res_local` — 9 records — with the `G_LI12_5_KO`/`_EN`/`_JA`/`_ZHS`/`_ZHT`
  pattern, except that `CONTENTS15_4`, `15_5`, `15_7` reuse the **bare** `G_LI15_4`
  for KO/EN/JA/TC and only SC gets `_ZHS`); 10 records keep a single non-empty `res`
  (`CONTENTS12_1`..`12_4`, `CONTENTS13_1`, `CONTENTS14_1`..`14_4`, `CONTENTS15_1`,
  `CONTENTS15_3`); and `CONTENTS15_2`, `CONTENTS15_6`, `CONTENTS15_8` ship
  `res: ""` — an **empty string, key present**. No interpretation is attached; it is
  a shipped-byte fact and a reason not to treat `res` presence as a liveness signal.
- All five locales are present and non-empty for all 22 records. Across the **whole**
  2.8.0.1 body, no locale value is an empty or whitespace-only string, but exactly one
  locale map is **short a key**: `CONTENTS9_8.desc1` has `KO`/`JA`/`SC`/`TC` and
  **no `EN` at all** (see §2.5b). A client reading `desc1["EN"]` on that record
  misses; this is the only such hole in the corpus.

## 2.6 `CONTENTS15_8` — the one complete numeric table in the new block

Guilt Blitz (Guild Blitz) winning-point schedule, EN verbatim with markup stripped:

```text
Defense Team Deployment Points                        : 150 Points
Victory over Defense Team Points                      :  25 Points
…on the 1st Attack                                   : Plus 50 Points
…on the 2nd Attack                                   : Plus 25 Points
For Each Surviving Hunter after Victory              : Plus  5 Points
For Each Undeployed Unit during the Attack            : Plus  5 Points
For 1 Occupied Area                                   : 2250 Points (Double for 3 Final Defense Line Areas)
For Each Deployed Defense Team in the Occupied Area   : Plus 50 Points
```

Plus the two deployment-cap rules: the cap is decided by **the number of Guild
Members participating in the Blitz**, and — importantly — it is decided on
**the Guild with fewer participating members** of the two.

Why this is impure and what it is good for: it is a complete, internally consistent,
first-party point economy with **no located native consumer**, no server route in
this replica, and **no second version to diff against** (Guild Blitz does not exist
in 1.4.4.1). It cannot be validated, only recorded. Two arithmetic observations, both
labelled as such: the 2250 area point is exactly **450 × 5**, and `CONTENTS15_6`’s
**3000**-point reward floor is *below* one area's 2250 × 1 plus deployment points,
so it is reachable without taking the final defense line — a plausible design, not a
verified rule. Nothing here may be turned into a fixture.
