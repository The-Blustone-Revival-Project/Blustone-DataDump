{{Character|title1 = Myung-Sun|gender = Male|relations = Seo Hyun (daughter)|current_state = Alive|season(s) = 1 and 2|image1 = Myung-Sun.jpg}}Myung-Sun is the director of the worldwide known [[Fortune Seeker]] and one of the key characters of the game story.

It was because of him that the [[White Virus]] was discovered and the research about it began as he intended to create a better world to his daughter by using the virus to cure terminal illness like cancer.

He has been infected with the white virus in White Island and many attempts against him already happened and survived to the most extreme cases in the level to people don't believe someone could survive.
