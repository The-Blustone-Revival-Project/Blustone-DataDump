{{Season_Template|episodes_number = Unknow|playable_characters = Unknow|image1 = Game WI2 Title .jpg|release_date = Aug 26, 2010}}This game was the continuation of White Island before its small rework, now it can maybe be considered a sequel to [[White Island: Director's Cut]].

In this game, it was introduced alternative endings based on your choices which aren't used in the main game. Also, the player was going to control more than one character during the story to show its viewpoint of everything; similar to this we have the [[A Flower Unto Death|first Season]] which we control both [[Ji Hoon]] and [[Jae]] in different parts of the game showing some similar things in different viewpoints.
