==Welcome to the Wiki==
White Island is a mobile RPG game by [http://www.visualshower.com VisualShower Corp.] available for both [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vshower.wifhd.ww&hl=en Android] and [https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/white-island-season-2/id1031133996 IOS]. A mystery puzzle game with elements of horror, it progresses based on episodes, released periodically by the developers.

The game currently has 2 [[Seasons]] ([[A Flower Unto Death]] and [[The Broken Lead Heart]]) and has been released in 2007 in Korea and its global release came at 2011 with some elements of the game improved and reworked just as the addition of more puzzles, improved game mechanic and slightly changed story (the main difference is the addition of dialogues).
