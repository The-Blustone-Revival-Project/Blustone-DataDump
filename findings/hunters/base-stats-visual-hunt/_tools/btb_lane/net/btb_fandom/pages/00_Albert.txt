== Appearance ==
Albert is clearly a robot, with a smooth, black metal body and blue lights for eyes. It's always seen dressed in butler clothes - white shirt, grey vest, black bowtie and tailcoat, black pants and shoes. 

== Personality ==
Albert talks very formally, but does things at its own pace, not listening to others. It seems to have high respect only for [[Edison]], and can carry out horrifying tasks without blinking an eye. According to [[Phip]] and [[Belle]], Albert was responsible for trapping [[4994]]. It also trapped Phip in an electric barrier for no apparent reason except to 'make an example' so that no one approaches 4994. The reason for its actions are unknown, though it does seem to have its own protective reasoning behind encasing 4994.

== Position ==
Albert is one of the three [[Chief Administrators]] of Judecca, the other two being Amalgam, the master craftsman, and the soldier.

== Abilities ==
Albert is able to walk on air, an ability he demonstrates more than once. 

As a Chief Administrator, it is also able to activate barriers and give orders to other robots in Judecca, one of which is [[Noah]], who keeps 4994 encased.
