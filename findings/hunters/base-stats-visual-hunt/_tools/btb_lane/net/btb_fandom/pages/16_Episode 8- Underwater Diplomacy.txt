== Lakeside ==
So that was a bunch of conversation. Now finally time for the walkthrough!
* Pick up the fish bones and the fishing rod.
* Use the ''Fishing Rod'' to retrieve Lantern's book.
* Read ''Aeromechanics'' to the Dodo bird.
* Have Nene pull the bird out of the door.

=== Engine Room ===
* Head back out to the lake, and proceed down the path to the right.

=== Building Entrance ===
* Have the ''Goggled Dodo Bird'' open the door.
* Enter the building.

== Dodo Bird Conference ==
Fishy business, isn't it? (Pun wholly intended.)
* Go closer to the left wall.

=== Left Wall ===
* Try to get a meal from the machine.

* Return to the main hall, then ask any of the Dodo birds on either side about ''Meal Distribution Time''.
* Return to the Left Wall.

* Fiddle with the clock.
* Tap on the meal distributor.
* Have the ''Goggled Dodo Bird'' demand a meal.
* Use the ''Fish Bones'' to outwit the machine.
* Pick up the metron.
* Return to Lakeside.

== Lakeside ==
* Open the compartment of the small building on the left and put the ''Meal Distributor's Metron'' into it.
* Go into the open elevator doors.

== Underground Tunnel ==
* Have Nene eat the mushrooms the yellow fish is eyeing.
* Examine the 'fishes' on the right.

=== Dodo Bird Conference ===
Sheesh, talk about hot-headed.

There's nothing to do here for now, so return to the Lakeside.

== Lakeside ==
* Retrieve the metron from the elevator.
* Imbed the ''Meal Distributor's Metron'' into a mushroom, then put that together with the ''Fishing Rod.''
* Throw the ''Fishing Rod with Mushroom'' into the lake.
* Combine the ''Fishing Line ''with the last ''Big Mushroom'' in your inventory.
* Return to the conference.

== Dodo Bird Conference ==
* Go up the stairs on the right to the curtain.
* Tie the ''Fishing Line with Mushroom'' to the curtain's tassel.
* Go back to the main hall.
* Talk to the Great Bird.
