== Appearance ==
Nene has golden eyes and hair, although her hair ends are blue. She has brown bear ears although she's of the Polar Bear Tribe; it is unclear if she has human ears. Nene has a tattoo on her left arm and seemingly her left thigh as well.

True to one of the Polar Bear Tribe, Nene wears very revealing clothes. Even when she is seen to be wearing a coat, she leaves it unzipped. She does, however, keep the orange-red striped scarf and goggles on. She wears a green pendant as well.

== Personality ==
Nene is rash, and deals with things aggressively. Despite her rashness, she listens to [[Lantern]] (as much as her patience allows). Perhaps due to her polar bear lineage, she prioritises her stomach and what she enjoys rather than thinking and acting logically. Nene is easily provoked. She does make a good pair with Lantern, however, as they make up for what the other lacks; Nene's strength is invaluable in many situations. She is also optimistic, sometimes overly so.

== Abilities ==
Nene can communicate with any and all animals freely and perfectly; it is thus unclear if the animals such as the Dodo bird(s) can talk in the human language or if Nene is translating all of their speech. While Lantern seems to be talking to the animals directly, it could easily be a hassle-saving effort for both the game makers and the players. 

With Nene's strength, she can also lift absurdly heavy things, whether to clear paths or to fight.

Nene seems to have a knack for hip-hop, and is seen correcting [[Leonardo|Leo]]'s words with a rhyme, leading to the latter calling her a 'hip hop bear girl'.
