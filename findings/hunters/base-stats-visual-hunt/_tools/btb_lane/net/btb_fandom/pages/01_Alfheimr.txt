"A small village located between the Sapiens Tribe and the Polar Bear Tribe. It's a wasteland due to its proximity to the [[Central Pole]], making it the coldest place on the entire continent. It became a village of machinists when [[Edison]], one of the two Wise Men, settled there. All the machines and devices from Alfheimr are highly regarded all over the world for their fine engineering and superior craftsmanship. The village is cold all year long, but for three months of the year no one can enjoy outdoor activities at all due to the brutal cold and blistering snowstorms. And yet, even in this hostile environment, the village has survived thanks to a gigantic central heating system developed by Edison and the engineers who maintain it. Nobody knows how Edison came to settle in such a place, but rumor has it that the secret is connected somehow with the [[Legend of the Two Jewels]]." 

- From [http://btb.visualshower.com/world_en the official Beyond the Bounds website] .
