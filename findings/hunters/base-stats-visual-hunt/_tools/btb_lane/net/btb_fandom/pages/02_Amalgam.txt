== Appearance ==
Amalgam is an AI, or an artificial intelligence. While it does not look like a conventional one, [[Belle]] reveals that Amalgam is a robot, while she advised [[Lantern]] to have Amalgam make a promise with it, saying that 'Robots cannot lie'. 

Lantern first meets Amalgam as a small, blue jewel trapped in the hands of rebelling robots. Amalgam's central body is merely a larger version of the extracted body, with one larger orb in the middle of the jewel body and 3-6 smaller orbs around it.

== Personality ==
Amalgam is extremely prideful, with no qualms in referring to itself as the 'greatest artificial intelligence in all of Judecca'. Because of its pride, when things turn out different from what it predicts, it is either quick to panic, or becomes defensive. 

It is also quick to take credit, showing its vanity and perhaps narcissism.

== Position ==
Amalgam is one of the three [[Chief Administrators]] of Judecca, the other two being Albert, the butler, and the soldier.

== Abilities ==
Amalgam is known as the master craftsman as it allegedly made everything in Judecca, although Belle adds that the worker robots were the ones who built them from Amalgam's blueprints. 

In the maze, Amalgam is able to draw out maps of the floor or area they're in, even being able to pinpoint obstacles and collapsed rooms. 

At the start of [[Episode 8: Underwater Diplomacy|Episode 8]], Amalgam was also able to warp the gang out of the maze and into the train. It was also able to gain access into the train and communicate that way.
