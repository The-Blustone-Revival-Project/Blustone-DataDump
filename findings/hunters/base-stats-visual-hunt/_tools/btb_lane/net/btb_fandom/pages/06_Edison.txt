One of the two Wise Men, and the man who turned [[Alfheimr]] into a village of machinists. He wrote 'Aeromechanics', [[Lantern]]'s bible, and developed the town's central heating system. Though his appearance has yet to be revealed, Lantern has been mistaken for Edison by the robots of Judecca, and according to [[Amalgam]] and the other machines, Lantern and Edison have a high match rate of 98.6% in their biometric data. Edison is the only person [[Albert]] listens to. He also has some relation to [[4994]], although the exact relationship is unclear.
