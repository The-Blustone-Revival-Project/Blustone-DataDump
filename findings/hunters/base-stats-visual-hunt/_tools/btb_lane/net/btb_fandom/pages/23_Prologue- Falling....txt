The first instalment of the game!

This episode, or prologue, is pretty much a tutorial, so there's frankly not much need for a walkthrough.

I'm doing it anyway!

Again you'll get the steps in the tutorial, but here's a rundown of the mechanics if you're new to VisualShower's games.

When you are able to explore, or when no characters are talking, the bottom of the screen functions as your inventory. Items you've picked up or assembled can be found there.

There are three buttons - for BTB, the 'Act' button is also the 'Observe' button, represented by a hand and a magnifying glass. You can observe your surroundings, or look at the items in your inventory in more detail. To assemble items, you'll have to tap and hold onto one item until the 'Assemble' option flashes, then drag the item to the second item that you want to combine together with the first. At any one time, you can only assemble two items. The 'Move' button is self-explanatory, represented by footprints. However, you can only move to designated places, which will have flashing, moving footsteps, similar to the footprints representing the button. The last button 'Communication' is represented by two speech bubbles. Again, it's self-explanatory, but it can be useful to get extra information when you're stuck and have nothing else you can do.

You'll get the hang as we go along! And so it begins...
