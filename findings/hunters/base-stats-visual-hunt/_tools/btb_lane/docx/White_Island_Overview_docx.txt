White Island

Mystery thriller with a supposedly highly rated story
Two primary modes -> Exploration (item combining, item usage, conversation with stranger, puzzling) + Story (text conversations)
Two seasons so far. Season 1 is done, ~$27 Season 2 is halfway and ~$10
Ratings: 5* (10,000) | 4* (2,000) | 3* (1,000) | 2* (600) | 1* (11,000)
Compared to The Secret of Monkey Island by the developers
Assets and story derived from the original White Island trilogy

























Blustone

Different than the other English releases to date
Open ended with events and both active/passive connect with other players
A mash between tapper/clicker games and RPG elements
Loads of background story for each character, the ability to ask them about their background and what not
Assets and story derived from BTB: The Legend of Blue Stone


























Beyond the Bounds

Similar to White Island in style, also runs the White Scripting Engine
Season 1 is about halfway and costs ~$10
Precedes Blustone
A lighter adventure than White Island
More character driven at a guess since White Island is a bit more dull as far as character writing? Not a confirmed fact yet
Assets and story derived from the original Beyond the Bounds trilogy
