TERMS Attacker: Unit with high speed, high attack, but low hit points and defence. Usually paired with critical chance/damage based Bombers or Support units. May also mean Attacker team in Fight Club. Block Chance: Stat used widely to negate most of the critical damage done by units. It also greatly weakens the performance of special attacks of Attackers and Bombers, as it lowers the maximum normal and critical damage hits. (Example: Reduction of Block chance through Hermes critical special move). Block Resistance: Stat used widely to work on reducing most if not all of the damage caused by auto attacks of all elements, through the use of blocks. It’s effectiveness is linked to block chance; it also affects special attacks. Example: Edward/Erise/Lawrence 5th skill + Therapy combination with proper jewels (4 x Super Block + 2 x Block) will give an extremely high block resistance. There is a special unit that can ignore this stat completely (Genovia) Bomber: Hunter with very high special attack, mediocre life points and defensive stats. Cannot use it’s special movement if it’s on the frontline and the fifth skill needs to be properly combined to maximize your frontline’s performance. Combined Power: A synonym of Total Power/Combat Power/CP/TP, it directly links the strength of your units to the amount of gathered Attack, Defensive power, and Health, along with other sub-stats and modifiers (Transcendence, equipment, fates, jewels, collection bonus…) Critical Damage: Stat which greatly increases the maximum damage done and received by auto attacks and special movements, but that can be easily negated by increasing the block chance/resistance stat of a unit. Currently the most relevant offensive trait in the game. Damage Calculation: The way system will display the current damage you are causing and receiving after taking into account all the primary game stats (For more information, check this topic made by the user Cayenne Pepper/ 카이엔페퍼 : https://bs.visualshower.com/share_en/1494773 ) Defender: A very high defensive stat based foe or ally, usually with very high life points, and that can serve to block powerful special movements. However, it has low damage output and speed. Example: Gerard, Captain. Defensive: The general protective ability of all units, it reduces the vulnerability to special attacks of the element a hunter is weak against, and the damage taken from bleeds / automatic attacks of all elements. It may also mean Defensive jewels: Using these will increase the healing power of support units and other special hunters like Patrone or Nene/Lottery Mouse/Lunar Alia. By last, it will also mean a Defensive Fight Club set-up. Elemental Resistance: Refers to the trait of a unit to answer better against the element that it is weak against. By enhancing this power, your hunters will become more resistant against their usual counter methods. (Ex. Anemone versus Bella or Lapis) Enrage: The rate at which special movement bar fills up, some units can greatly increase it, while others can slow it. Example of adders: Celine, Captain. Example of removers: Vincent, Lottery Mouse. Fodder: Refers to units which are typically used as either training material for core deck units, or as advancement purposes (Recommended). They’re usually extra copies of 1* and 2* hunters. It can also mean Blitz units that are expendable but that can get the objective done (also called trash units) Guild: Association of people with common interests for a determined goal or objective (For Blustone’s case, limited to 40 people) Hybrid: A mix of units that can serve for many purposes. It’s diversity allows for the capability to reply to enemy movements. It has a combination of units of all kinds (Attackers, Defenders, Supports, Bombers) with the objective to reduce the amount of weaknesses and augment the power to deal with different decks. They’re also considered a synonym to Spear Decks [Mainly due to these decks being able to be used both as Offensive or Defensive decks). Example: Genovia + Captain + Sienna + Lottery Mouse + Westfield Offensive: Can refer to the type of jewel, or to the style of fighting of a deck, which has as priority dealing as much damage as possible and that can or not have a healer available. Snare: Ability displayed by some hunters like Harley, Dino, Pansy or Gullveig. Makes your base speed 0 during the special attack duration and greatly reduces the damage of a special movement. Not to be confused with Stun, as Snare still allows for character’s swapping Stun: Special movement displayed by certain units like Diana, Anemone, Lawrence or Windblade. Makes the unit become unable to attack or swap out, therefore, easy to knock out if not healed or countered by another stun or a snare. Some of these moves are special attacks impossible to remove (Example: Fire Lawrence) Support: Unit with medium - high life points, medium - high defence, low damage output, whose main task is to keep team units alive. Will not use it’s special movement if it’s on the frontline. Can ALSO mean Customer Support for bugs / issues solutions. Stall: Teams based on the mix of solely Defenders (Tanks) + Healers (Supports), that try to maximize Block Chance, Block Resistance, Health, Enrage gain and Defensive stats in general, to try and time-out win against users who fight versus these decks in the Fight Club or Blitz. Example: Little General Anemone + Captain + Leon + Celine BASICS 1. - What is VisualShower? VisualShower is the name of a South Korean company that makes mostly mobile games. Genovia or Lottery Mouse, as their utility in both Cruise, Excavations and Fight Club-Guild Blitz is quite extensive. (Considering I’m a new user) If you feel that the time you have currently spent in the game is not enough and that you are still on time to restart, feel free to do it. This game depends a lot on the first hunters you manage to collect, and they will be priority on if you feel actually interested to continue in the game or not. If you are not pleased with them, you can make another account and try to pull scrolls again. PVP AND EXCAVATION/CRUISING 4 . - In which order should I be making Master Hunters (5*'ing)? For Offensive: Genovia > Captain (or Lottery Mouse/Celine) > Audrey/Sienna > Westfield. You can work with those you currently have, and leave those not recruited yet for later. Some people tend to add Alia or Leon (latter one especially for Blitz), as their special abilities help beat / nullify the opponent set ups and reduce / nullify special moves considerably too. For a proper Cruise team, you should go for 1 attacker + 1 (2) bomber (s) +1 (2) healer (s) (Example: Heidi + Lantern + Diana + Joy). Current best cruise deck is 1 attacker + Dumble + Crocus + Leon. This will also apply to Abyss composition teams. Try to always prioritize Offensive. ABYSS SET-UP EXAMPLE: Try out Windy + Alia / Celine / Selene + a Selene / Celine co-op if available. The damage and speed of this set-up is awesome for ice-based ruins / abyss! General principle: There will always be people who will be able to break your defences, but you must be able to train your Offensive to be able to break theirs too (Otherwise, they will camp you over and over again in the Arena, and your Guild Blitz will also risk being lost). 5. - What is the optimal Offensive PvP set-up? For latest patch (2.6.5.0) The most popular one is Windblade/Genovia/Vincent, paired with either Sienna / Audrey for bombers, + Westfield/Celine/Lottery Mouse. Some people replace Celine / Westfield with Captain for a reliable frontline back up, at the expense of losing some team buffs. Some people use 1 high healing based healer on their offensive with a single attacker, instead of 2 attackers. (In this case, the best learner friendly support+bomber option is Alia+Selene, which can help prevent accidents and mistakes ). If you get to recruit and transcend Genovia, ALWAYS consider when to use her skill, as her 4th ability + unremovable second and fourth skill uses are nice to have an advantage from the start of the fight. It will also work as a good method to prevent constant regenerative abilities. Genovia is considered the second best Pay To Play (and the first one for F2P users) unit along with Vincent, due to her versatility, reduced amount of weaknesses and secured recruiting method (4* selection scrolls), so she’s one of the primary units to focus on getting / upgrading / spending your jewels on. 6. - What is the optimal Defensive PvP set-up? For latest patch (2.6.5.0) For free to play users, the most effective one is to go for Gerard + Captain + 3 Healers (Westfield + Celine + Clara) for maximum stalling purposes . If you want to have your enemy risk his ticket by overpowering him , you can go for Christmas Mary / + Genovia + Celine + Audrey + Sienna 7. - What considerations should I take into account when selecting a hunter to train? First of all: Always keep 1 copy of each unit so you are able to 5* them for Fate bonuses! For Fight Club purposes: Almost all the 1* are to be discarded (except for Captain, Tyltyl and Balian, as they’re still good when awakened), unless you want to have the extra units for Guild Blitz, as they can be a nice complement. All the 2* are to be used as low risk units in defensive or offensive purposes for Guild Blitz. They are not viable for any serious pvp team set up. The 3* units are pretty solid as part of a defense or offensive as long as they’re accompanied by at least one 4* unit. Do not use them in Fight club, however, as you will not have enough raw power to break through defensive teams. As a reminder for Guild Blitz, all units are viable and are suggested to learn to use for both offensive and defensive. TRANSCENDENCE AND MANDA 8. - What is Transcendence? EQUIPMENT AND GEMS 22.- What is equipment? How do I upgrade it? The equipment is a feature first unlocked when a hunter reaches the Proficient Hunter degree (4 stars). It’s technically a stat enhancer feature. It can prove to be very effective in terms of making your units get that last bit of strength to beat either PvE or PvP content. It’s divided in 3 pieces: Weapons will augment your Attack Power. You will typically want to raise this as a priority in both your Attackers and Bombers. A rmor will augment your Defensive Power. You will typically want to raise this as a priority in both your Defenders and Healers. Accessories will augment your Life Points. You will want this on all your units, but it will have greater effects on your Attackers and Defenders, as they’re the main units you want to have in the frontline for either PvE, and PvP offensive/defensive battles. Toolboxes and Advanced tool boxes are materials to upgrade your equipment with, providing a small stat bonus. However, their cost increases with each tier, and can be very expensive in the long run You can purchase the equipment’s upgrade materials from the Mirage Shop, using manda, or by slowly gathering them from completing the Mirage Ruins. The special element modificators (Coolants, Flammable materials, Lightning boxes) are used every time you get an equipment piece to levels 10, 20, 30 and 40 (Each requiring more rupees and amount of them than the previous). Be sure to stock well on these, as you will require huge amounts past level 20. Please note that this is directly connected to the future available jewels themselves. The higher degree your equipment is, the more slots you will unlock to be able to place them on. For more information about equipment, please check this link out: http://bs.visualshower.com/en/849114 “First, what is Equipment? - There are 3 types of unique equipment that a hunter wears. - It consists of Weapons, Armor, and Accessories. - Each Equipment cannot be replac ed and can be upgraded individually. What is Equipment upgrade? - Upgrading each Equipment’s ability makes a hunter stronger. - To upgrade Equipment, you need rupees and a Toolbox. - But, the maximum upgrading level depends on the five-level modification level. - You can easily get Toolboxes by playing in Mirage Ruins - Additionally, you can buy them from Mirage Shop or Manda Shop. ** In the future, you can acquire equipment modification materials instead of skill training materials. What is Equipment modification? - When the equipment upgrade level is 10, 20, 30, 40, it can be modified. - When modifying equipment, the equipment ability increases, and it expands the maximum upgrading level higher. - To modify Equipment, you need modification-specific materials instead of Toolboxes. - Modification materials require different materials depending on the element of the hunter. Fire: Flammable Material / Ice: Coolant / Electricity: Lightning Box - Modification materials are available when you clear stage 3, 6, 9 in Mirage Ruins. - Additionally, you can buy them from Mirage Shop or Manda Shop. What are the Equipment slots for? - Unlike Equipment, Jewels can be detached and have their own unique abilities, so you can enhance each of your hunters in the direction you want. - Equipment comes with 1 slot by default. Upon reaching level 20, an equipment gains an additional jewel slot. - Jewels are classified into five classes. The higher the class, the more power it has. - The class of Jewels is determined at the time of acquisition, and the class itself cannot be changed.” Stated by VisualShower’s / Blustone’s moderators.

Jewels are technic ally skill / stats enhancers for your hunter units. Their effects can stack up, and they are the last push up available. They are currently only obtainable through manda / ruins, and you will not be able to get mileage gems through free claim anymore (as it was not supposed to happen originally) - There’s many types of them, but you will always want to prioritize those that benefit your offensive team (Being your Attacker, the one who comes first, it must be provided with the best equipment possible). A lot of combinations are available, it’s up to you for what you want to make, but I suggest basic ideas, like adding lots of block chance+reduction / defense jewels to your tanks (4 super block + 2 block or 6 defensive ones working the best on Juno / Junia/ Lawrence and Harley for ex.) and, in the case of offensive, to go for pure raw power or speed to breach through all kind of defence setups / PvE content (For example, if your enemy has a very reduced defensive stat or no block chance/elemental resistance at all (or a hybrid deck), you will want to dedicate your 5* gems to Critical / Fatal / Offensive jewels on Twins/Genovia and Speed/Elemental Enhancement / Offensive on Windblade). If you want to go for a Jack of All Trades, you can use the gems that focus on boosting overall ATK and Block Chance/DEF. When you do a 10 gems pull (250 manda cost), all the gems that you get will be focused on the skill set you picked for them. META Effects Set-UP (Assumes the user manages to deploy 3* or higher jewels): 4 x Speed/Elemental Enhancement/Fatal Jewel + 2 x Offensive Jewel (Attackers). For best performance, read the skill set of the unit you will be equipping these jewels on. Preferably ATK or Critical Damage + Chance main stat (top one) + sub stats (all those below the first one) 6 x Offensive for ALL bombers (can use 4 Elemental Enhancement + 2 x Offensive on Selene, Sienna, Audrey, Lottery Mouse) with elemental enhancement / ATK sub stats 2 x block + 4 x superblock (Defenders) - In Anemone/Calibur’s case, they use 6 x Defensive.. Preferably DEF + block chance / elemental resistance substats. If your enemy has Genovia as main attacker, it’s best to just use 6 defensives, as damage reduction gets ignored by her special abilities. 6 x Defensive Jewels for all healers, with Defensive + Elemental Enhancement substats on them. Some 4* jewels may have better substats than natural 5*; this is important if you are someone who’s really selective on their way of picking the best stuff for units. Equal gem sets activated will always prevail over having individual type gems, so ensure that your units do always get the 2-3 effects of the jewels you add! Single jewels do NOT give any kind of effect (For example, 1-3 Speed jewels will NEVER give you the set effect, so beware!) Explanation: As you can see, the HP, Offensive and Defensive gems are more centered in creating a Jack of All Trades unit, one with balanced stats, or, in such case, to try to help them fix their weaknesses (You may want to use these on your bombers/healers). Also, if you have to prioritize critical damage over chance or viceversa, always pick critical chance, because damage has an actual cap, and it won’t work half of the time if your base chance is poor to begin with. Critical and Fatal Jewels will help you against defensive set ups or PvE content where the block chance is low enough for it to not matter, or, give that extra punch to special hunter cases like Chichi (who has very high attack power by herself) or Twins/Genovia (who have a 5th skill related to critical damage or damage reduction ignoring). Block chance jewels will benefit all tanks, but Lawrence and Junia (who are usually the second/third tank in a stall set up) will get the most benefit out of it as long as Genovia is not involved in countering them. Super Block Jewel will work as a sort of block resistance effect (but since this stat is the most impacting in terms of current game’s defensive performance, you should be encouraged to use them on units that are not the main target of a Genovia user [Especially Blitz], as you will want to improve the survivability of all your tanks) Element Damage Enhancement Jewel will increase the performance your unit already has against an enemy weak to their element, in terms of how well they counter it. This is a good investment on Selene, and Crocus, for cruise purposes, and Windblade, to counter Vincent and Gerard. Speed jewels will work great to increase the attack speed of all units. However, they do not increase the skill gauge rate. Regeneration and Elemental Resistance jewels are currently not recommended in any kind of strategy, and by so, should be avoided. Penetration jewel will make your units do 1 extra damage per every % you raise it, and it works just like Vincent’s skill effect. It can be pretty handy against high block resistance based teams. It is considered a direct counter to super block jewels, but not as great as buffing ATK, EE, or Critical Chance/Damage. Jewels can be refined, and this process can make them more appealing to use, as it also makes the stat reward higher, and by so, makes your team members stronger. However, the higher the level a gem is, the higher the chance to fail refining it will be. (It can be very frustrating and money/time consuming after level 9+, so be sure to count with lots of resources before attempting to do so, at least 600k rupees). GUILDS AND CONTRIBUTIONS 24 .- What is a Guild? What do I need to do to join one? What are contributions? A guild is essentially the pursuit of certain objectives through the association of people with similar interests or traditions. In the case of Blustone, they are designed to increase the interaction between communities in game and provide great rewards. The guilds are divided into three kinds: Private (Exclusive by invitation) On Permission Only (Can request joining, but it will be up to the Leader / Supervisor to review and accept / deny the application) Public (No requirements. Essentially, Free For All) Some of these guilds will also ask for a certain score in the Fight Club before being able to join. This score can range from 0 up to 4,000 points. Leaders can set the: Flag, Name and Description of the Guild, Score requirement, appointment of supervisors or new leaders. Some of these details can be modified for a fee paid in Manda. Supervisors have almost the same permissions as Leaders, however, they cannot modify any of the Guild’s descriptive information. - Contributions are made through participation in the Fight Club (each win guarantees you 300 points, while defeats are 250) or by using your Guild Cooperative partners on ruins / searches (Roughly 75 points each). - Donation in rupees is an alternative way to contribute to the guild, however, this is extremely expensive, and it’s NOT recommended at all. - Leaving a guild will mean you will be unable to rejoin a new one for a day. If you are planning to do Guild Blitz, it’s recommended to leave just after the rewards stage has just finished.
