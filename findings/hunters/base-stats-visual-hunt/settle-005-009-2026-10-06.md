`戦闘力`, printed on the same page as an independent quantity, is **invariant to
`貫通力`** and **sensitive to `ブロックダメージ減少量` / `HP再生力` /
`致命打ダメージ量` / `属性強化` / `属性耐性`**. Every pair above either matches that law or
shows `攻撃スピード` as an additional non-`戦闘力` field. `005`/`009` sit exactly on it.

**A copy-paste by the transcription lane would have produced an identical `貫通力` as
well. It did not.** Two independently-read pages produced two rows that differ in
precisely the one field the site's own `戦闘力` ignores. That is corroboration of row
integrity independent of my reading the `基本能力値` table.

**Negative, recorded so it is not over-read:** `戦闘力` gives **no** leverage on the ATK
*magnitude*. An ordinary-least-squares fit of `戦闘力` on hp/atk/def over the 62 numeric
rows has residual rms 607 on a mean of 8767 (6.93 % of scale); substituting `576` for
`628` moves the fitted value by only 68.8. I therefore do **not** claim `戦闘力`
independently proves 628. It proves row integrity; the `基本能力値` block proves the number.

## 7. Archive traps hit, recorded per `GUARD-encoding-and-archive-traps.md`

1. **CDX / availability returned a reproducible FALSE NEGATIVE for the `009` URL.**
   `https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=<exact>&fl=…` returned **HTTP 200 with an
   empty body** in three encodings — exact URL with uppercase percent-hex, exact URL with
   lowercase percent-hex, and a prefix match on `…/レアリティ別/キャプテン*`. The same
   URL's `id_` fetch returns **HTTP 200 / 34 670 bytes**. The `005` and `011` CDX queries
   worked normally on the same host, minutes apart. So a CDX zero here means "the index
   did not list it", **not** "not archived" — a second, independent instance of the guard's
   archive trap, and the reason the task's exact-URL instrument was the correct one.
2. **`id_` at a timestamp with no exact capture is unreliable in both directions.** The
   `009` request at `20191015065805id_` returned **404 on one attempt** and a correctly
   resolved **200 → `20191211113041id_`** on a later attempt, minutes apart. Far-timestamp
   probes (`20200101000000`, `20191001000000`) returned 404. **A 404 from an `id_` request
   is not proof of absence**; only a 200 with a body counts as evidence here.
3. **Egress was the dominant cost.** `web.archive.org` (207.241.237.3) refused TCP for
   most of the session (`WinError 10061`, and `HTTP 429` when it did connect). Positive
   controls: `archive.org` and `example.com` answered normally at the same moments;
   `wayback-api.archive.org` answers `302` and redirects into the blocked host;
   `web.archive.org.wstub.archive.org` connects but returns `500`;
   `web.archive.org` via other IA IPs in 207.241.237.0/24 either time out, are refused, or
   present a certificate that fails hostname verification and were correctly rejected.
   TLS verification was never disabled for a content read. Recoveries took 30–45 s
   spacing between requests.
