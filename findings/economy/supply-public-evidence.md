2026-10-04. Public browsing plus bounded preserved-guide snippets only.
No server/client/source edits, adb/Ghidra, credential access or messages to others.

## Result: no original Supply quantity/timer recovered

This pass did **not** verify an official Supply claim amount, claim interval,
daily reset time, VIP quantity or final2.8.0.1 Supply screen from public material.
It would be inaccurate to turn generic stamina conventions or current local
fixtures into original balance. Public sources do support fuel as a resource,
Manda restoration and historical login-bonus terminology, but not those numbers.

## Historical secondary / firsthand evidence

[MrGuider's Blustone guide](https://www.mrguider.org/guides/blustone-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategy/)
shows publication/update wording datedJanuary1,2023. It states Manda can restore
fuel. It provides no fuel amount, AP maximum, Supply cooldown or daily rollover
at that statement. This is secondary gameplay guidance, not publisher-authored
policy, and its date alone does not prove it was retested against2.8.0.1.

[WriterParty's Blustone guide](https://writerparty.com/party/blustone-top-tips-cheats-and-strategy-guide/)
has a search-indexed historical snippet describing Supplies as the place for
login bonuses. Full page retrieval failed. Treat the name **Supplies** and
login-bonus association as a discovery lead, not a verified timer/quantity or
proof that the final client uses the identical old interface. Search metadata
places it about2017, well before the final APK and later Blustone2 changes.

[July8,2020 firsthand fuel thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/Blustone/comments/hndswl/that_a_lot_of_fuel/)
contains players reporting3500 received fuel and holdings6486, with another
reported high7900. These are historical player comments. They **do not identify
Supply as the award source**, do not establish natural AP cap behavior, and
provide no claim/reset timer. The linked image was located but its pixels could
not be retrieved (HTTP403); no screenshot values or UI labels are claimed as
visually observed. Large holdings are not a license to guess overflow mechanics.

[Omega Play's December26,2019 Blustone post](https://www.omegaplay.net/2019/12/blustone-o-game-mais-simples-e.html)
is retrievable and describes firsthand play. It is a potential historical video
creator lead, not official VisualShower material. This pass did not obtain a
Supply claim sequence or video frame with quantity/timer. YouTube search-page
retrieval was throttled; no timestamped video observation is claimed.

## Observed versus unverified

Observed in accessible text: publisher attribution; fuel restoration with
Manda in a secondary guide; historical players discussing large fuel holdings;
community fuel-spending/event vocabulary. Verified local native request/schema
work remains the separate implementation oracle.

Still unverified publicly: Supply claim amount, natural fuel recharge rate/cap,
claim cooldown versus fixed daily windows, daily reset timezone, over-cap award
behavior, VIP bonus formula, paid-refill cost, and final2.8.0.1 button/timer labels.
No50/100/150 quantity, three-hour/four-hour interval or midnight reset was adopted.

The useful next historical capture would show an original Supply screen before
claim (label/amount/timer), the AP value before and after, then its post-claim
countdown and known app/date context. Even that would need VIP/account context
before interpreting a base award or final-version parameter. Until then, preserve
explicit fixture labels for the revival's quantity/timer and use native parser
contracts to make claims persistent and idempotent without claiming original
balance fidelity.
