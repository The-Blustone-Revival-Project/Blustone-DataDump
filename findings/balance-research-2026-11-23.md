# Reading the November balance observations

The strongest evidence for starting HP is the same fresh party entering three Midgard Snowfields battles in September 2019 footage. Before contact, the protagonist shows 173, 175 and 177 HP, while Tyltyl shows 156, 157 and 158. These are full effective battle HP readings and can include party effects. Each clear gains one Hunter level. Each entry costs six Fuel. The settled Rupee totals are 56, 54 and 54 after the victory counter finishes. Team-level bonuses appear separately. Each area has one observed clear. ## Follow-up measurements

The same recording follows the starting Captain from one star at level 27 (Strength 556) through training to level 30 (586) and advancement to two stars at level 1 (799). A different Hunter, Hermes, has a dated three-star level-one panel with 637 HP, 507 ATK and 358 DEF. At five stars and level 30 his displayed totals are 3,187, 1,977 and 1,227. The later jewel panel explains part of the difference. The equipment screen has a one-point ATK subtotal discrepancy. These observations constrain a progression curve while leaving individual core-stat growth open.
