- **Set:** Master of Counterattack
  - Weapon: **Steel Boots** — Thanks to the weight, the wearer gets to train their leg strength every day. They enhance the destructive power of the Flying Kick.
  - Armor: **Dragon Design Armor** — Although it is for ornamental purposes, it can sometimes be useful when receiving a powerful attack.
  - Accessory: **Signboard** — The contents are unknown, but Monica always carries it for the spirit of the letters.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `081|TITLE`, `081|SUMMARY`, `081|WEAPON`, `081|WEAPON_DESC`, `081|ARMOR`, `081|ARMOR_DESC`, `081|ACCESSORY`, `081|ACCE_DESC`, `081|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
