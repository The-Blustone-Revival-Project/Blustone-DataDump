  - Game-wide rule for cross-checking [COMMUNITY-SOURCED
    `namu.wiki/w/블루스톤/헌터`]: 3 Rage for Offense/Defense, 4 Rage for
    Bomber/Support; Bear and Lily & Lyla cost 4 even though they are not.
    Rage capacity is **10** and does not scale past that.
  - [UNKNOWN] The literal `[value]` / `[duration]` magnitudes are **not**
    shipped — only the formula. Per the in-game text `[value]` resolves as
    `(value + value_up * skill_level)`, so the server must synthesise
    `value`, `value_up` and `duration`. Skill levels 1..5 are real:
    `skill_1_level`..`skill_5_level` are native keys (see
    `analysis/recovered-data/native-key-vocabulary/deck-and-arity.tsv`).
- **Set:** Twilight Bat Clan
  - Weapon: **Fire Bat** — A fire bat that fumes flames when energy is accumulated. This fire bat and its owner both have hot characteristics.
  - Armor: **Gangster Hoodie** — The official zip-up hoodie that the Twilight Bat Clan wore when they ruled the shady streets. To the world, it is a symbol of fear.
  - Accessory: **Skull Necklace** — According to Rebecca, it is the cutest skull necklace in the world.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `021|TITLE`, `021|SUMMARY`, `021|WEAPON`, `021|WEAPON_DESC`, `021|ARMOR`, `021|ARMOR_DESC`, `021|ACCESSORY`, `021|ACCE_DESC`, `021|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
