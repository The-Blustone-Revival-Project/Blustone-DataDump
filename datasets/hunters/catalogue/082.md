- **Set:** Little Santa
  - Weapon: **Santa's Scythe** — Although Santa Force changed it to look like wings of an angel, its sharpness is still the same.
  - Armor: **Santa's Dress** — A cute dress that portrays the happiness of Christmas and Mary's innocence.
  - Accessory: **Santa Fedora** — This fedora decorated with Christmas ornaments adds an extra level of stylishness as the finishing touch to Mary’s Little Santa concept.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `082|TITLE`, `082|SUMMARY`, `082|WEAPON`, `082|WEAPON_DESC`, `082|ARMOR`, `082|ARMOR_DESC`, `082|ACCESSORY`, `082|ACCE_DESC`, `082|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
