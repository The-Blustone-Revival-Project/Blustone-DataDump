- **Set:** Firepower Maniac
  - Weapon: **Sienna Cannon** — A massive weapon that gave up on caring about weight and size and only focused on firing power and beauty.
  - Armor: **Thermal Turtleneck Sweater** — A turtleneck that was invented coincidentally during an invention of a weapon and earned a lot of money.
  - Accessory: **Heatproof Cannon Gloves** — Gloves that protect you from extreme heating of the cannon. It is also an essential item when you are holding hot pot handles.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `020|TITLE`, `020|SUMMARY`, `020|WEAPON`, `020|WEAPON_DESC`, `020|ARMOR`, `020|ARMOR_DESC`, `020|ACCESSORY`, `020|ACCE_DESC`, `020|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
