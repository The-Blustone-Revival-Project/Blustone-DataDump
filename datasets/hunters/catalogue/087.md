  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `087|TITLE`, `087|SUMMARY`, `087|WEAPON`, `087|WEAPON_DESC`, `087|ARMOR`, `087|ARMOR_DESC`, `087|ACCESSORY`, `087|ACCE_DESC`, `087|VICTORY`.

    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
