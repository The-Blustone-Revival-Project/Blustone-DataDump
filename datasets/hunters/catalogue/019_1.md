- **Set:** Thunderbringer
  - Weapon: **Thunder Sword** — A strange sword covered with deadly sparks, but it doesn’t seem to harm Balian.
  - Armor: **Spike Jacket** — A jacket that Balian wears to show his masculinity. Is it effective though?...
  - Accessory: **Romantic Fanny Pack** — A bag that has a ring inside so that Balian can be ready to propose anytime. Just seeing it makes Hecate freak out.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `019_1|TITLE`, `019_1|SUMMARY`, `019_1|WEAPON`, `019_1|WEAPON_DESC`, `019_1|ARMOR`, `019_1|ARMOR_DESC`, `019_1|ACCESSORY`, `019_1|ACCE_DESC`, `019_1|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
