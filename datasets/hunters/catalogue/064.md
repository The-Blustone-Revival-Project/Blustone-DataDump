- **Set:** Artifact Collector
  - Weapon: **Whip Sword** — A sword from ancient times with a flexible blade that bends like a whip. No one can escape its wrath.
  - Armor: **Black Uniform** — For Geranium who thinks highly of formal wear, this is essential! It may seem uncomfortable, but he assures that it is quite the opposite.
  - Accessory: **Forbidden Suitcase** — A suitcase containing things not meant for the world to see. There are reserved for Geranium’s eyes only.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `064|TITLE`, `064|SUMMARY`, `064|WEAPON`, `064|WEAPON_DESC`, `064|ARMOR`, `064|ARMOR_DESC`, `064|ACCESSORY`, `064|ACCE_DESC`, `064|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
