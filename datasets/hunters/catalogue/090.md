- **Set:** Owner of Saw Blade
  - Weapon: **B.F. Chainsword** — One of the B.F. series, a legendary ancient weapon series. It is as large and beautiful as its outrageous power of destruction.
  - Armor: **Cast Iron Gauntlet** — This gauntlet protects Katrina's hand when using the Chainsword. It seems strong, but when you look at the Chainsword, it feels like the gauntlet needs to be stronger.
  - Accessory: **Saw Blade Sneakers** — Erise pointed out saying, "Do you have to put a saw blade even in your shoes?" However, it didn't have any effect.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `090|TITLE`, `090|SUMMARY`, `090|WEAPON`, `090|WEAPON_DESC`, `090|ARMOR`, `090|ARMOR_DESC`, `090|ACCESSORY`, `090|ACCE_DESC`, `090|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
