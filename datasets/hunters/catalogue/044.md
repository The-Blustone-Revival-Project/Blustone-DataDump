- **Set:** Girl with Two Faces
  - Weapon: **Big Rabbit Pillow** — We need to blame this pillow for its extreme comfort. Seeing as Heidi falls asleep as soon as she lays her head on it, unleashing Hide.
  - Armor: **Microfibrous Pyjamas** — Pajamas with superfine fibers that are very soft and warm. As soon as you wear them, you softly fall asleep.
  - Accessory: **Pompom Headband** — A headband you use to pull your fringe back when you are washing your face. But it is actually so tight that it makes your head feel numb.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `044|TITLE`, `044|SUMMARY`, `044|WEAPON`, `044|WEAPON_DESC`, `044|ARMOR`, `044|ARMOR_DESC`, `044|ACCESSORY`, `044|ACCE_DESC`, `044|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
