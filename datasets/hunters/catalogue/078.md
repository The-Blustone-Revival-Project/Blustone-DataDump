- **Set:** Ghost Bride
  - Weapon: **Ghost Bride’s Bouquet** — It has a gloomy vibe that could make even a perfectly healthy person fall ill.
  - Armor: **Ghost Bride’s Dress** — The dress shows Teresa’s will to overcome life and death for her true love.
  - Accessory: **Ghost Bride’s Veil** — When the bride and groom are similar in height, this veil makes the bride look taller from the back view.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `078|TITLE`, `078|SUMMARY`, `078|WEAPON`, `078|WEAPON_DESC`, `078|ARMOR`, `078|ARMOR_DESC`, `078|ACCESSORY`, `078|ACCE_DESC`, `078|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
