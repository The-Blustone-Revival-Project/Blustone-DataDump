- **Set:** Eternal Flame
  - Weapon: **Ultra-High Temperature Plasma Sword** — A sword emitting ultra-high temperature plasma with the power of the Burning Heart. It possesses destructive power of another level.
  - Armor: **Red Combat Coat** — A red coat that Captain has been wearing since he woke up. It is very light and warm, so many people want it.
  - Accessory: **Union Epaulet** — The mark of the Union, which was the most powerful faction on Earth a thousand years ago. Its shape symbolizes the Earth and the solar system.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `009_1|TITLE`, `009_1|SUMMARY`, `009_1|WEAPON`, `009_1|WEAPON_DESC`, `009_1|ARMOR`, `009_1|ARMOR_DESC`, `009_1|ACCESSORY`, `009_1|ACCE_DESC`, `009_1|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
