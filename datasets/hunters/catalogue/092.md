- **Set:** Monster Sniper
  - Weapon: **Monster Sniper Rifle** — Upon closer inspection, it resembles a cannon more than a rifle. The core part is made of an ancient relic, which makes mass production impossible.
  - Armor: **Reinforced Exoskeleton Suit** — The inside of the suit is full of metal devices that disperse tremendous recoil during shooting.
  - Accessory: **Large-Caliber Special Bullet** — Although having a large-caliber bullet, it is difficult to deal fatal damage to monsters. Its main purpose is to weaken the thick armor.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `092|TITLE`, `092|SUMMARY`, `092|WEAPON`, `092|WEAPON_DESC`, `092|ARMOR`, `092|ARMOR_DESC`, `092|ACCESSORY`, `092|ACCE_DESC`, `092|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
