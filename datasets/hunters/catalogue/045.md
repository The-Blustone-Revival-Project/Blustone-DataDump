- **Set:** Ice Skating Fairy
  - Weapon: **Skate Shoes** — They seem like an ancient weapon as there's a dangerously sharp blade. But, they also help you move quickly on ice.
  - Armor: **Figure Dress** — It is said that Yuri has handmade this outfit as a result of being inspired by a legendary figure skater's costume.
  - Accessory: **Chocolate Syrup** — Delicious chocolate syrup. You must always have this in hand as you need to have something sweet after practice.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `045|TITLE`, `045|SUMMARY`, `045|WEAPON`, `045|WEAPON_DESC`, `045|ARMOR`, `045|ARMOR_DESC`, `045|ACCESSORY`, `045|ACCE_DESC`, `045|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
