- **Set:** White Darkness
  - Weapon: **Ancient Boomerang** — This is a boomerang equipped with ancient technology. It always returns to Selene, no matter where she is.
  - Armor: **Old Bodysuit** — It’s severely damaged and worn out, but it still provides minimal protection.
  - Accessory: **Hybrid Gloves** — These gloves were reinforced by ancient technology. They protect her hands when she catches her boomerang as it returns.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `068|TITLE`, `068|SUMMARY`, `068|WEAPON`, `068|WEAPON_DESC`, `068|ARMOR`, `068|ARMOR_DESC`, `068|ACCESSORY`, `068|ACCE_DESC`, `068|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
