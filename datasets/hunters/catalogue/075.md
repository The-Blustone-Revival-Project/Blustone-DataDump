- **Set:** Dancing Sword
  - Weapon: **Sword Dance Swords** — A pair of swords releases intense cold as Bella's dance gets faster. Since it's white and beautiful, it looks like it's for ornamental purposes.
  - Armor: **Dancing Clothes** — Adds to the beauty of dancing, but the fluttering ornamental fabric confuses the enemy.
  - Accessory: **Blue Skirt** — A fancy blue ornamental fabric. It's amazing how Bella doesn't trip over this during her sword dance.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `075|TITLE`, `075|SUMMARY`, `075|WEAPON`, `075|WEAPON_DESC`, `075|ARMOR`, `075|ARMOR_DESC`, `075|ACCESSORY`, `075|ACCE_DESC`, `075|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
