- **Set:** Master of Recruitment
  - Weapon: **Gleipnir** — The ultimate tool to put someone in a sack. Nobody can escape once they get caught.
  - Armor: **Dark Hoody** — A hoody so black that once you wear it in the dark, nobody can recognize you.
  - Accessory: **Pure Gold Chain Necklace** — The only things in Lottery Mouse’s head are pure gold, business, and success.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `084|TITLE`, `084|SUMMARY`, `084|WEAPON`, `084|WEAPON_DESC`, `084|ARMOR`, `084|ARMOR_DESC`, `084|ACCESSORY`, `084|ACCE_DESC`, `084|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
