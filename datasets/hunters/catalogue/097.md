- **Set:** Santa Twins
  - Weapon: **Santa Lily's Batons** — You can tell by the looks that scream wouldn't even come out when you get hit by these heavy batons. It's too practical to be a cheering product.
  - Armor: **Santa Twins’s Clothes** — It may be called Santa's clothes for convenience, but it actually is a highly efficient combat uniform with bulletproof and heat protecting functions.
  - Accessory: **Santa Lyla's Cheer Pom Poms** — Cheer Pom Poms that Lyla shakes. It doesn’t have any healing effects, but Lily’s injury gets recovered!
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `097|TITLE`, `097|SUMMARY`, `097|WEAPON`, `097|WEAPON_DESC`, `097|ARMOR`, `097|ARMOR_DESC`, `097|ACCESSORY`, `097|ACCE_DESC`, `097|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
