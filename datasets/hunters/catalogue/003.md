    `skill_1_level`..`skill_5_level` are native keys (see

- **Set:** Walking Disaster
  - Weapon: **Metal Pipe** — A weapon perfect for ignorant Nene... was what Lantern said and was gone for a few days.
  - Armor: **Door Shield** — A shield that looks like a door. Whenever Lantern hears that it looks like a door, he shows a sad face.
  - Accessory: **Mandarin Scarf** — A scarf she took from Lantern during their childhood days. To Nene it's a memory, to Lantern it's a scar in his heart.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `003|TITLE`, `003|SUMMARY`, `003|WEAPON`, `003|WEAPON_DESC`, `003|ARMOR`, `003|ARMOR_DESC`, `003|ACCESSORY`, `003|ACCE_DESC`, `003|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
