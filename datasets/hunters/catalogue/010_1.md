- **Set:** Despair of a Millennium
  - Weapon: **Ultra-Low Temperature Plasma Sword** — The sword emitting ultra-low temperature plasma with the power of the Frozen Heart. It emits extreme cold, making it difficult to approach.
  - Armor: **Purple Combat Coat** — A combat coat that has winterproof, windproof, and bulletproof functions. It's slightly older than Captain's, but it is presumed to be the same.
  - Accessory: **Damaged Union Epaulet** — The mark of the Union, which was the most powerful faction on Earth a thousand years ago. It has suffered extensive damage, making it difficult to recognize any features.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `010_1|TITLE`, `010_1|SUMMARY`, `010_1|WEAPON`, `010_1|WEAPON_DESC`, `010_1|ARMOR`, `010_1|ARMOR_DESC`, `010_1|ACCESSORY`, `010_1|ACCE_DESC`, `010_1|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
