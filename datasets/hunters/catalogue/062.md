  - Weapon: **Monkey Wrench** — It is widely accepted that this wrench is the largest monkey wrench in the world.
  - Armor: **Protective Goggles** — You need to protect your eyes! Your eyesight is precious! So, always keep your distance!

  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `062|TITLE`, `062|SUMMARY`, `062|WEAPON`, `062|WEAPON_DESC`, `062|ARMOR`, `062|ARMOR_DESC`, `062|ACCESSORY`, `062|ACCE_DESC`, `062|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually

    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
