    `namu.wiki/w/블루스톤/헌터`]: 3 Rage for Offense/Defense, 4 Rage for

    `analysis/recovered-data/native-key-vocabulary/deck-and-arity.tsv`).
- **Set:** Experiment 2
  - Weapon: **Magic Wand** — A toy that Sunny used to play with when she was young. She was holding it tightly in her hand when she woke up in this world.
  - Armor: **Surgical Gown** — A gown she wore her entire life living as an experiment. Because she was always given the same size, it has become too small for her now.
  - Accessory: **Chocolate Bar** — This chocolate bar is Sunny's favorite snack in the world. Someday in the future, history will change because of this chocolate bar.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `022|TITLE`, `022|SUMMARY`, `022|WEAPON`, `022|WEAPON_DESC`, `022|ARMOR`, `022|ARMOR_DESC`, `022|ACCESSORY`, `022|ACCE_DESC`, `022|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
