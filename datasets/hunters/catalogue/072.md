- **Set:** Dangerous Gambler
  - Weapon: **Crossbow** — A powerful weapon, but it is difficult to handle. According to Audrey, getting hit or not depends on luck.
  - Armor: **Red Rider Jacket** — You can see Audrey's taste of the vivid red color, unlike the general Werewolf tribe.
  - Accessory: **Gambling Dice** — This perfectly balanced gambling dice is near- professional with its exact probability to roll 1 and 6.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `072|TITLE`, `072|SUMMARY`, `072|WEAPON`, `072|WEAPON_DESC`, `072|ARMOR`, `072|ARMOR_DESC`, `072|ACCESSORY`, `072|ACCE_DESC`, `072|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
