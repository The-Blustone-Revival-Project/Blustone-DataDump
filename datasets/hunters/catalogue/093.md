- **Set:** Midsummer Sword Dancer
  - Weapon: **Inflatable Dual Swords** — Although it doesn’t seem threatening at all, Bella's sword dance allows it to cut anything.
  - Armor: **Dancing Swimsuit** — Swimsuit made by Erise, resembling Bella's Dancing Clothes. It was made very sturdy for dynamic movement.
  - Accessory: **Red Veil** — A splendid decorative cloth tinged with red. Weirdly, Bella doesn't trip over it during her dance.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `093|TITLE`, `093|SUMMARY`, `093|WEAPON`, `093|WEAPON_DESC`, `093|ARMOR`, `093|ARMOR_DESC`, `093|ACCESSORY`, `093|ACCE_DESC`, `093|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
