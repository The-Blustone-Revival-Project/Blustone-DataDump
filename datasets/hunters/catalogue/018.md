# 018 — Robin "King of Survival"

- **Identity:** Robin "King of Survival"
  - Display name (EN): Robin
  - Korean: [UNKNOWN] — the KO column of `CHAR_STR_RO` is present but the

  - "What it is": a playable **Defense**-class hunter (헌터). The four
    roles are Offense / Defense / Bomber / Support; the five *races* are a
    separate axis (Human/Sapiens, Werewolf, Coffee Rabbit, Polar Bear, Animal).

  - Game-wide rule for cross-checking [COMMUNITY-SOURCED
    `namu.wiki/w/블루스톤/헌터`]: 3 Rage for Offense/Defense, 4 Rage for
    Bomber/Support; Bear and Lily & Lyla cost 4 even though they are not.
    Rage capacity is **10** and does not scale past that.
  - [UNKNOWN] The literal `[value]` / `[duration]` magnitudes are **not**
    shipped — only the formula. Per the in-game text `[value]` resolves as
    `(value + value_up * skill_level)`, so the server must synthesise
    `value`, `value_up` and `duration`. Skill levels 1..5 are real:
    `skill_1_level`..`skill_5_level` are native keys (see
    `analysis/recovered-data/native-key-vocabulary/deck-and-arity.tsv`).
- **Set:** King of Survival
  - Weapon: **Metron Dagger** — A multi-purpose dagger that can be used for ice wall climbing, climate and humidity checking, a compass and even as a hand warmer!
  - Armor: **Thermal Vest** — The design looks dull but the inner structure is well designed. The King of Survival does not need to care about fashion.
  - Accessory: **Skull Eye Patch** — An eye patch that Robin has been wearing since we don't know when. There seems to be a secret to his left eye.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `018|TITLE`, `018|SUMMARY`, `018|WEAPON`, `018|WEAPON_DESC`, `018|ARMOR`, `018|ARMOR_DESC`, `018|ACCESSORY`, `018|ACCE_DESC`, `018|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
