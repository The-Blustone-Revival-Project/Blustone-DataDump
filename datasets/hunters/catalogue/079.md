- **Set:** Defense Knight
  - Weapon: **Knight’s Longsword** — Simple looking, but it actually is a masterpiece beyond price, made of ancient metals.
  - Armor: **Extra Large Tactical Shield** — This large shield is the core of a Strongpoint Defense Knight. It is overwhelming for an offense.
  - Accessory: **Deflection Armor** — Due to having a large and strong shield, armor is just decoration for a Strongpoint Defense Knight.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `079|TITLE`, `079|SUMMARY`, `079|WEAPON`, `079|WEAPON_DESC`, `079|ARMOR`, `079|ARMOR_DESC`, `079|ACCESSORY`, `079|ACCE_DESC`, `079|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
