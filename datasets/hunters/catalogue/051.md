    `value`, `value_up` and `duration`. Skill levels 1..5 are real:
    `skill_1_level`..`skill_5_level` are native keys (see

- **Set:** Guardian of Fight Club
  - Weapon: **Chicken Feet** — It is said that chicken feet are much more delicious than they may appear to be... But anyway, Chicken Feet is a threatening weapon.
  - Armor: **Chicken Wings** — Rich in collagen and protein, the whole family's favorite food! ...Is not what it is. It is actually an armor that protects Beckhen's body.
  - Accessory: **Jack** — Jack's main role is to be Beckhen's interpreter, but sometimes he helps in the battle. ...Says Beckhen.
  - All of the above are [RECOVERED] `CHAR_STR_RO` keys `051|TITLE`, `051|SUMMARY`, `051|WEAPON`, `051|WEAPON_DESC`, `051|ARMOR`, `051|ARMOR_DESC`, `051|ACCESSORY`, `051|ACCE_DESC`, `051|VICTORY`.
    NOTE: these are *named pieces*, not set bonuses. The game has **no**
    gear-set bonus system — each hunter owns exactly one weapon, one armor
    and one accessory, upgraded individually
    [COMMUNITY-SOURCED `Blustone Guide FAQ.md` L301-L346].
  - Gear -> stat mapping [COMMUNITY-SOURCED FAQ L303-L305]:
    Weapon -> Attack Power, Armor -> Defensive Power,
    Accessory -> Life Points.
