## Measured at: **Lv.30, no equipment**

This distinction is the whole point. The stat block shows the card's **own** numbers
in white and the **equipment + jewel** contribution in green. Proof that the reading
is correct and not a guess:

| check | arithmetic | result |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | nude `24068` + gear `+6291` | `= 30359` |
| card page (fully geared) | Strength `30359` | **exact match** |

The card page shows Strength **30359**; the equipment screen shows base `24068` with
`+6291` in green. 24068 + 6291 = 30359 exactly. The white column is therefore the
nude card and the green column is the gear. [DERIVED from an exact identity, and the
identity is what makes it safe]

A second, independent confirmation of the green column: the six jewel sub-stats
sum **exactly** to it — Elemental Resistance `1.44 + 1.69 = 3.13` ✓, Critical
Damage `3.13 + 3.87 + 3.86 = 10.86` ✓, Damage Reduction Block `0.50 + 0.38 + 0.50 +
0.38 = 1.76` ✓, Critical Chance `0.67` ✓, Elemental Enhancement `1.65` ✓,
Block Chance `0.72+0.56+0.56+2.30+0.56 = 4.70` ≈ `4.69` ✓ (rounding),
DEF `102 armor + 11 + 11 + 29 = 153` ≈ `154` ✓ (rounding),
HP `260 accessory + 58+21+58+18+21 = 436` ≈ `437` ✓ (rounding).

## Confidence and caveats

- Identity, level and rarity are certain (exact string match + printed level).
- Every number above was **read by eye at magnification** as well as by OCR, and
  both agree. Tesseract alone misread `33.00` as `"e .00"` on this very image, which
  is why nothing here rests on OCR alone.
- **Community figures may be pre- or post-balance.** The FAQ screenshots are dated
  by their content (recruitment of 3★ scrolls, patch 2.0.5.0 referenced as "most
  recent as of 22/12/2018"), so these are **2018–2019 era** values. Treat as a
  snapshot, not as the 2.8.0.1 balance.
- This is a **player card**. It says nothing about monsters.
