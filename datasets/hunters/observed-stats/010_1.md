### Two corrections that were nearly shipped wrong

1. **Strength is `61815`, not `671815`.** Tesseract returned `671815`. Visual
   inspection at 5x shows `61815`. The OCR digit was wrong and was discarded.
   This is the clearest illustration in this round of why an OCR'd number without
   human confirmation is worse than no number.
2. Card page values are **as displayed**, which may already include equipment.
   Unlike card 051 there is no green/white split on this screen, so these are
   **not** known to be nude base stats. [UNKNOWN] whether gear is attached.

## Comparison with card 051 — the one structural finding of this round

Both cards are **Offense / Ice at Lv.30**, so the `LEVELING.jet` role multipliers
cancel and the ratio is meaningful:

| | Beckhen `051` 3★ | Vincent `010_1` 5★ awakened | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| HP | 2879 | 4980 | **1.730** |
| ATK | 1471 | 2634 | **1.791** |
| DEF | 1149 | 1992 | **1.734** |
| Strength | 24068 | 61815 | **2.568** |

- HP / ATK / DEF scale by a **near-constant scalar (~1.73–1.79, 3.5% spread)**.
  That is consistent with rounding, and it suggests the three combat stats share a
  **single per-card scalar** over a common shape — so one number per card, not
  three independent ones, would reproduce them. [DERIVED, n=2, weak]
- **Strength does NOT follow that scalar** (2.57x). Whatever drives Strength
  weights rarity and/or skill level separately from HP/ATK/DEF. Note Vincent's
  skills are 50/50 while Beckhen's are unknown, so skill level is a live
  confound. **[UNKNOWN]** — do not model Strength as a function of HP/ATK/DEF.

Two data points cannot separate "rarity" from "level curve" from "skill level".
Treat the scalar as a hypothesis, not a law.
